VERIFIED ACCREDITATIONS

Global environmental & quality standards

Our manufacturing facilities and yarn supply chains undergo rigorous independent annual third-party audits.

GOTS Certified

CU 884729 / Organic Cotton

Ensures organic status of textiles from harvesting of raw materials through environmentally and socially responsible manufacturing.

GRS Certified

Global Recycled Standard

Verifies recycled content across cotton, wool and polyester while tracking chemical management and ethical labor standards.

RCS Certified

Recycled Claim Standard

Provides third-party chain-of-custody verification of recycled raw materials through every spinning and knitting transition.

RWS Certified

Responsible Wool Standard

Guarantees that wool comes from farms with a progressive approach to managing land and animal welfare (strictly non-mulesed).

ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY

Cleaner production, renewable energy, and circularity

We actively minimize our carbon footprint, water usage, and textile waste across all stages of yarn handling and garment finishing.

Rooftop Solar Renewable Energy

Solar installations supply daytime clean energy to our ASDWA facility, reducing grid reliance.

Water Recycling & Eco-Finishing

Closed-loop washing systems filter and recycle process water, eliminating harmful chemical effluents.

Pre-Consumer Fiber Recovery (LYK)

100% of our clean fabric clippings and yarn residues are reclaimed into regenerated yarns via our LYK platform.

SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

Empowering the people behind the craft

Our 2,500+ workers are the true core of Fashion Comfort Group. We cultivate an ethical environment that prioritizes healthcare, family support, and professional equity.

Free On-Site Healthcare & Medical Clinic Full-time certified physicians and nursing staff provide free clinical consultations, medicines, and prenatal care for workers and their families.
Subsidized Daycare & Child Development Center Safe, hygienic, educational childcare facilities on-site allow working mothers to maintain their careers with peace of mind.
Living Wages & Continuous Skill Training Fair remuneration exceeding national statutory minimums, paired with regular technical upskilling on computerized knitting machinery.
COMMUNITY & BIODIVERSITY

Regenerating ecosystems and local communities

We take proactive responsibility for our local environment through community agroforestry, tree plantation campaigns, and educational assistance.

Over 5,000 indigenous trees have been planted across our factory perimeters and local flood-vulnerable zones, enhancing microclimates, sequestering carbon, and creating restorative natural greenery for our team members.
