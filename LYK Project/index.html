THE CIRCULAR PROCESS

A circular system, not just a marketing claim

A closed-loop industrial infrastructure designed to keep valuable fibers circulating indefinitely through European fashion supply chains.

01

Optical Color Sorting

Post-industrial cutting waste is categorized strictly by fiber type and original shade, eliminating the need for subsequent wet chemical re-dyeing.

02

Mechanical Garnetting

Clean cutting scraps undergo gentle mechanical carding to preserve natural fiber length, preventing pilling and maintaining yarn tensile integrity.

03

Regenerated Spinning

Reclaimed fibers are precision-blended with certified GOTS organic cotton or lyocell to create consistent Nm counts suitable for 3GG to 12GG machines.

04

Traceability by Design

Every yarn lot receives a Digital Product Passport (DPP) tracking original cutting batch origins, GRS transaction certificates, and LCA metrics.

Regulatory Alignment

Why circularity matters now

The global fashion industry is pivoting toward mandatory recycled content, driven by upcoming EU Ecodesign (ESPR) directives and brand carbon targets. LYK gives our partners an unfair advantage in compliance and sustainable storytelling.

By controlling our own spinning and fiber sorting pipelines, we shield our clients from virgin commodity price volatility while guaranteeing genuine pre-consumer authenticity.

Fiber Value Retention

Keeping luxury fibers in continuous circulation, reducing waste and preserving the embedded energy of combed cotton and wool.

Reduced Virgin Dependency

Less reliance on volatile external virgin crops, establishing a resilient circular ecosystem inside our own industrial group.

EU Regulation Readiness

Engineered directly in compliance with European Union ESPR (Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation) and upcoming Waste Framework standards.
