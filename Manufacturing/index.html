Zero-Defect Philosophy

Quality control & consistency

Quality is systematically integrated into every phase of our operations, from raw yarn cone intake to container loading.

STAGE 01

Inward Yarn Testing

Laboratory inspection of yarn count, tensile strength, twist per inch, and shade lot continuity.

STAGE 02

Panel-by-Panel Inspection

Every knitted body and sleeve panel is light-table inspected for dropped stitches or tension variance before linking.

STAGE 03

Precision Linking

Skilled seamstresses hand-link collars, cuffs, and side seams with exact pitch alignment.

STAGE 04

Wash & Shrinkage Audit

Controlled washing, tumbling, and measurement tests to verify zero skewing and standard hand-feel.

STAGE 05

Dual Needle Detection

Mandatory 9-point conveyor metal detection on all garments to meet strict European baby & child safety rules.

STAGE 06

Final AQL 1.5 Audit

Independent QA team performs full AQL random sampling on packed cartons before export sign-off.
